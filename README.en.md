## 📔 About & Safety Notes

This repository curates legitimate discounts and free perks available to verified students across software, AI tools, cloud services, hardware, and travel. **Please protect your student status—never sell, rent, or resell your educational accounts**, as abuse leads to stricter verification for everyone.

> **Last Full Verification: November 2026**. Pricing, regional eligibility, and verification rules may change over time—always double-check the official landing page before applying. Found a new offer or an outdated entry? Open an issue via our [Issue Templates](https://github.com/ivmm/Student-resources/issues/new/choose)!
>
> *Note: This English edition includes **globally available resources** only. For Mainland China local perks, see [README.md](README.md).*

* **Usage Terms**: See [Usage & Attribution Notes](https://github.com/ivmm/Student-resources/blob/master/license). When updating global offers via PR, please sync changes across other language READMEs if possible.
* **Privacy & Security**: Be cautious of unofficial sites requesting government IDs or holding-ID selfies. For official scholarships or financial aid, always consult your university directly.
